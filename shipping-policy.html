Shipping Policy

Order Processing

Orders are typically processed and shipped within:

  • 48 hours if the item is in stock.
  • Up to 72 hours if additional stock preparation is required.

As order volumes increase, inventory will be prepared in advance to help ensure timely fulfillment.

Worldwide Shipping

We proudly ship worldwide.

Delivery Times

Estimated delivery times begin once your order has been shipped. International shipping involves customs clearance, local courier operations, weather conditions, and other factors beyond our control. Delivery estimates are therefore provided as guidance only. Please refer to your shipment tracking information for the most accurate delivery updates.

Taxes & Fees

The price displayed at checkout is the final amount charged by COLWYN for your order.

Tracking

Every order includes shipment tracking. Track yours anytime on our Track Order page.

Contact

Shipping questions can be sent to our support team — see the Contact page.
