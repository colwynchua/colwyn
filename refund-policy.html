Refund Policy

We stand behind the quality of every order.

Damaged, Defective, or Missing Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or missing items, please contact us within 3 days of receiving your package. After verification, we will provide either a free replacement or a full refund, at no additional cost to you.

Lost Shipments

If your shipment is confirmed lost by the final-mile delivery provider, we will immediately arrange either a replacement or a full refund.

Returns

Due to the high cost of international shipping, we are unable to accept returns or provide refunds for:

  • Change of mind
  • Personal preference
  • No longer wanting the product
  • Ordering the wrong item

We appreciate your understanding.

How to Request a Refund

Contact us within 3 days of receiving your order, with your order details, via our Contact page.
